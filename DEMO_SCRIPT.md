# MaapSure five-minute winning demo

## Opening

“A weighing machine can affect a vegetable seller, a hospital patient, a farmer or an entire truckload. The machine is digital, but the official testing workflow still depends on repeated entry, manual checking and scattered evidence. MaapSure turns that into one controlled record that can be independently approved and publicly verified.”

## Live flow

1. On the sign-in page, click **Tester** and sign in.
2. Start a test and show the visible OIML clauses beside the readings.
3. Show the nine calculated checks and the ten conditional applicability decisions.
4. Save the controlled draft.
5. Try **Submit for review** before adding evidence. MaapSure blocks it.
6. Add a photo or PDF, then submit.
7. Sign out. Click **Reviewer** and open **Review queue**.
8. Add a review note and approve. Explain that the tester cannot approve their own work.
9. Download both the locked PDF and editable Word copy.
10. Open public verification. Point to the tester, independent reviewer, rules version and SHA-256 fingerprint.
11. Open **Audit trail**. Show that every action is linked to the previous one and the chain verifies from genesis.
12. If time remains, open **Standards & access** to show immutable rule versions and governed change requests.

## Strong finish

“MaapSure does not ask AI to make a legal decision. It uses visible, versioned formulas for measurement, a different officer for approval, permanent evidence and public tamper checking. AI only explains patterns. The department stays in control.”

## Honest answer to ‘is this government certified?’

“No student project can certify itself. The controlled workflow is built and tested. Statutory deployment still requires validation by the authorized Legal Metrology authority, security testing, approved hosting, official digital-signature integration and departmental acceptance.”
