# MaapSure five-minute demo

## The opening

"A weighing machine can affect a vegetable seller, a hospital patient, a farmer or an entire truckload of goods. The machine may be digital, but its official test report is still prepared through repeated manual entry and calculations. MaapSure changes that."

## The live flow

1. Sign in as the laboratory administrator.
2. Open **Start a new test**.
3. Select the RetailPro 30 instrument.
4. Continue with the prepared readings and show how MaapSure calculates a pass.
5. Go back and change the 30 kg indication to `30.026`.
6. Calculate again. MaapSure will fail that load point and explain the likely calibration problem.
7. Finalize the report.
8. Download the PDF to show the calculations and QR code.
9. Scan the QR code or open the public verification page.

## The line to finish with

"MaapSure does not replace the Legal Metrology Officer. It removes repeated paperwork, prevents calculation mistakes and gives the officer a complete evidence trail for every decision."

## Questions judges may ask

### Is the AI making the legal decision?

No. The OIML rules engine makes the calculation. The explainable diagnostic assistant finds patterns and suggests what an inspector should check. The authorized officer remains responsible for the final decision.

### How do you stop fake certificates?

Every finalized report has a unique verification code and QR link. The public page shows the stored certificate number, instrument serial number, test result, laboratory and date.

### Can it work on a phone?

Yes. The interface is responsive. An inspector can capture test photographs directly from a phone and use browser voice dictation for notes.
