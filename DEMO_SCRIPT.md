# MaapSure five-minute SIH demo

## Before the judges arrive

- Run the unit, integration and build checks and keep their latest output.
- Use the clearly marked **synthetic SIH demonstration** case.
- Keep a downloaded PDF as a venue-connectivity backup.
- Do not say “every OIML rule is covered,” “government certified,” “cryptographically authentic” or “production ready.”
- Call the counts a **prototype mapping inventory**: 34 report entries, 73 family records and 149 detailed prompts.

## 0:00–0:35 — the problem

“Model approval for a weighing instrument is much more than one error calculation. It combines long physical tests, equipment traceability, construction and software examination, evidence and independent review. When that work is split across paper, spreadsheets and folders, missing or changed information is difficult to see. MaapSure demonstrates how that file can become one governed digital case.”

## 0:35–1:15 — the model decides the plan

1. Sign in as **Tester**.
2. Open the type dossier.
3. Point to class, capacity, ranges, indication method, power, interfaces, software, tare, mobility and rolling-load features.
4. Open the evaluation plan and show an applicable path and a rules-derived not-applicable reason.

Say:

“The current catalog maps 34 R 76-2-aligned report entries, 73 project-defined R 76-1 families and 149 detailed examination prompts. Applicability comes from the declared model features, so skipping is visible and reasoned. We still require an authorized expert to validate this mapping clause by clause.”

## 1:15–2:05 — show automatic work and expert work separately

1. Open **Weighing performance**.
2. Point to the load, indication, fractional additional load, zero error, corrected error and MPE.
3. Briefly show the electrical-disturbance and environmental tabs.
4. Open the construction/checklist area and show its expert notes.

Say:

“For entered observations the current engine uses visible deterministic logic, including `P = I + 0.5e - ΔL`, `E = P - L`, and `Ec = E - E0`. Eleven family records derive from linked test results. Sixty-two families and the detailed prompts need expert disposition. AI does not make the legal PASS/FAIL decision.”

Then add:

“The software records EMI, damp-heat and endurance results; physical laboratory equipment performs those tests. Before real use, the authority must also approve the exact current IEC/ISO/Indian test editions and setups.”

## 2:05–2:45 — use the synthetic case honestly

Load or open the synthetic demonstration data and point directly to its warning.

Say:

“These readings are generated only to exercise the screens, calculations and workflow. They are not measurements from an instrument, and this is not a model-approval certificate.”

Show save/resume, the equipment register, evidence link and an incomplete-item blocker. Explain that a real case can take days.

## 2:45–3:35 — prove governed review

1. Submit a completed demonstration case.
2. Show that the Tester cannot approve their own work.
3. Sign in as **Reviewer**, add a note and approve.
4. Point out that the server recalculates at submission and approval.

Say:

“The approved snapshot includes the dossier, rules, entered observations, evidence manifest and review. Corrections do not silently rewrite the issued snapshot; they follow the revision and revocation flow.”

## 3:35–4:20 — show reports and integrity carefully

1. Download the controlled PDF and uncontrolled editable copy.
2. Show the mapped section summary, family matrix, detailed prompts and evidence hashes.
3. Open public verification and show the fingerprint match and revocation state.
4. Open the audit history.

Say:

“SHA-256 helps detect inconsistency against the stored approved snapshot and evidence bytes, and the audit entries are hash-linked. These are internal consistency checks, not a government digital signature or independent proof of issuer identity.”

## 4:20–5:00 — finish with the real innovation

“The innovation is a governed architecture for an overlooked process: model-driven applicability, deterministic calculations, explicit expert judgment, physical-evidence traceability and independent review in one case. We have made the standards mapping inspectable instead of hiding it in code. Our next gate is authorized validation, current external-standard editions and real laboratory comparison—not a false claim that a hackathon prototype certifies itself.”

## Safe answers to judge questions

### “Is every OIML rule covered?”

“We do not claim that yet. The prototype maps 34 R 76-2-aligned report entries, 73 project-defined R 76-1 family records and 149 detailed prompts. R 76-2 says its own checklist is only a summary. Our mapping now makes the remaining authority validation visible: clause-by-clause review, current external standards and physical laboratory trials.”

### “What is automatic?”

“The current engine deterministically evaluates structured measurement data and derives 11 family outcomes from linked tests. The other 62 families and the detailed prompts need expert disposition. Automatic arithmetic never proves that the physical procedure was correct.”

### “Does it perform EMI, humidity or endurance tests?”

“No. Qualified laboratory equipment creates those conditions. MaapSure plans the mapped path, records equipment and observations, evaluates implemented criteria and reports the result.”

### “Are the electrical tests current?”

“The branches are mapped from R 76, but production needs the authority to freeze and validate the exact current IEC/ISO/Indian editions, severities and setups. The prototype does not silently claim that external-standard update.”

### “Is the report authentic?”

“The prototype checks SHA-256 consistency and revocation state. Because those are unkeyed hashes, they are not issuer authentication. Government issuance needs approved digital signatures, protected keys and trusted timestamps.”

### “Is the data government-ready?”

“The hackathon version uses a controlled local JSON store. Production requires an approved encrypted database and object store, backups, disaster recovery, access monitoring and security approval.”

### “Is the passing demo real?”

“No. It is visibly synthetic and exists to exercise the full software path. Real claims require a qualified laboratory and authorized reviewer.”

### “Where is AI?”

“AI may explain an observation or find missing documentation. It is kept outside rules, readings, PASS/FAIL and approval because those need deterministic logic and authorized people.”
